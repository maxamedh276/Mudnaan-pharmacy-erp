03 / Try the unit logic

Box, strip iyo xabbo.
Isla medicine.

Category waa kooxda daawada. Dosage form waa nooca sida tablet, capsule, syrup ama cream. Selling unit waa waxa laga iibinayo: tablet, strip, box ama bottle.

Tusaalahan: 1 strip = 10 tablets, 1 box = 100 tablets. Unit kasta wuxuu yeelan karaa qiime iyo barcode u gaar ah. Conversions-ku waxay mar walba ku salaysan yihiin base unit-ka.

Demo xisaabeed oo keliya. Qiimayaasha hoose waa tusaale; ma aha prescription ama talo dose.

05 / People & responsibility

Shaqo kasta, access ku habboon.

Qaybintan masuuliyaduhu waa tusaale onboarding. Roles-ka iyo actions-ka waxaa lagu habeeyaa baahida farmashiyaha.

Owner / Manager

Overview, reports, staff iyo dib u eegidda xaaladda ganacsiga.

Pharmacist

Medicine setup, patients, prescription review, dispensing iyo batch decisions.

Cashier / Dispenser

Iibka loo oggolaaday, payments, receipt iyo dib-u-daabicid.

Storekeeper / Purchasing

Suppliers, purchase orders, batch receiving iyo stock counts.

Accountant

Customer/supplier balances, payments, journals iyo financial review.

Administrator

User access iyo settings. Operational Settings waxaa leh superadmin access.

Permission-aware navigation: prototype-kani wuxuu tusayaa dhammaan modules-ka si badeecadda loo fahmo. System-ka rasmiga ah wuxuu user kasta u muujiyaa bogagga iyo actions-ka uu oggolaanshaha u leeyahay.
06 / From presentation to rollout

Sida farmashiyaha loo bilaabayo.

  1. Faham catalog-ka, units-ka, staff-ka iyo stock-ka jira.
  2. Deji pharmacy identity, payment accounts iyo permissions.
  3. Diyaari medicines, categories, suppliers iyo opening balances.
  4. Hubi batches/expiry, conversions iyo qiimayaasha.
  5. Tababar receiving, mixed-unit sale, prescription iyo returns.
  6. Waafaji xogta bilowga, xaqiiji printing, kadib go-live.

Demo-ga aad macmiilka tusi karto

Ku bilow Medicines & Units; soo geli batch; iibi 1 strip iyo 5 xabbo; soo saar receipt; dib u daabac; ugu dambayn muuji expiry iyo balance reports.

Waxyaabaha macmiilku ogaanayo

Luqadda iyo dejinta waddanka

Core pharmacy labels waxay taageeraan English iyo Somali; finance/admin screens-ka laga dhaxlay ERP-ga intooda badan waa English. Currency, tax iyo payment accounts waa in loo habeeyaa ganacsiga. Shuruudaha waddanka waxaa xaqiijiya farmashiyaha iyo la-taliyayaashiisa.

Receipt, labels iyo printer

POS receipt preview, browser/PDF print iyo reprint ayaa jira. Prescription labels-ku waxay daabacaan directions-ka la diiwaangeliyey. Printer-ka dhabta ah iyo QZ setup haddii la isticmaalo waxaa lagu xaqiijiyaa qalabka macmiilka.

Prescription support

System-ku wuxuu taageeraa record, approval iyo partial/full dispensing quantities. Clinical judgment, dose selection iyo interaction assessment waxaa sameeya xirfadlaha; prototype-kani ma ballanqaadayo automated dosing, insurance integration ama e-prescribing integration.

Sidee loo wadaagaa bandhiggan?
