  1. English transcript

    Welcome to Mudnaan Pharmacy. This English walkthrough uses a separate training database and fictional records. Start at the sign in screen, enter your assigned username and password, and select Sign in. The password shown here belongs only to this temporary training session.

  2. English transcript

    The Home screen organises work into module cards. Select a module in the sidebar, then use the small tabs and sub tabs above the working area. Screens open underneath those tabs. Start with catalog setup, then suppliers, purchasing, receiving, dispensing, and reports.

  3. English transcript

    First, create a medicine category. Open Medicines and Stock, then Categories. Choose Add category, enter Training Medicines, and save. A parent category is optional. Categories help organise the catalog and make medicines easier to find.

  4. English transcript

    Open Medicines and Units and choose Add record. Create a fictional training tablet, select its category and base unit, and enter its generic name, strength, dosage form and manufacturer. For this demonstration the base inventory unit is one tablet. This record is for software training only.

  5. English transcript

    Set the tablet selling price to one dollar. Add a strip containing ten tablets, priced at eight dollars, and a box containing one hundred tablets, priced at seventy dollars. Every conversion refers directly to the base tablet. Save the medicine. Purchase costs will be entered separately.

  6. English transcript

    Open Purchasing and Suppliers List. Add Training Medical Supplies, with fictional phone, email and address details, then save. Supplier records connect purchase orders, received batches, bills, returns and payments.

  7. English transcript

    Open Purchase Orders and select New Purchase Order. Choose the training supplier and medicine. Order two boxes at a purchase cost of twenty dollars per box. Enter an order reference and expected receipt time, then save and confirm. The order total is forty dollars. Stock increases when goods are received.

  8. English transcript

    Open Goods Receiving and receive this order. Confirm two boxes, enter a batch number, and select a future expiry date. Manufacturing date is optional. Record the condition of the shipment and confirm receiving. Two boxes, each containing one hundred tablets, add two hundred tablets to stock.

  9. English transcript

    Open Batches and Stock. Check the medicine, batch number, expiry date, quantity and release status. Supplier purchases belong in Goods Receiving. The stock entry form is used for opening balances, authorised corrections, allocation of legacy stock and disposal. Expired, recalled or quarantined batches cannot be dispensed.

  10. English transcript

    Open Sales and Dispensing. Choose the training medicine, select strip, and enter one. Use Add loose or base units and enter five tablets. The summary shows one strip plus five tablets equals fifteen tablets. With the configured prices, the total is thirteen dollars. Complete a cash sale and inspect the result.

  11. English transcript

    Open Customers and create a fictional billing customer. Customer accounts are used for sales balances, receipts and statements. Patient records are separate and hold clinical information. After saving, inspect Customer Receipts. This screen collects unpaid invoices; our first sale was fully paid in cash.

  12. English transcript

    Open Patients and Prescriptions, then Patients. Add Training Patient with a fictional phone and date of birth. Record known allergies only when they have actually been supplied. Save the patient. Patient information is used when recording and dispensing a prescription.

  13. English transcript

    Return to the medicine record and enable Prescription required for this training item. Existing selling units remain unchanged. Save the record. Future sales of this medicine will require an approved prescription and a user with dispensing permission.

  14. English transcript

    Open Prescriptions and add a record for Training Patient. Enter a fictional prescriber, issue date, expiry date and reference. Choose the training medicine and prescribe ten base tablets. The training directions are placeholders, not medical instructions. Save the record, open its details and approve it.

  15. English transcript

    Open Dispensing Workspace and select the approved prescription. Choose the medicine in tablet units and dispense four tablets. Complete the four dollar cash payment. The prescription keeps a remaining balance of six tablets and becomes partially dispensed. Strip and tablet quantities are combined when checking prescription limits.

  16. English transcript

    Return to dispensing, select the same prescription and dispense the remaining six tablets. Complete the six dollar cash payment. Open Prescriptions again and verify that the status is Dispensed. A completed prescription does not authorise additional quantities.

  17. English transcript

    Open Sales Returns. Choose the original mixed unit cash sale and return one strip. Select the cash refund account and enter a reason, then post and confirm the return. The refund uses the original strip price. Returned medicines go into quarantine rather than immediately becoming available for sale.

  18. English transcript

    Open Batches and Stock and inspect the quarantined return lot. In the stock operation form choose disposal, select that return batch, and enter ten tablets with an authorised disposal reason. Save. The returned quantity is removed from physical inventory and the adjustment is recorded.

  19. English transcript

    Open Purchasing and Purchase Returns. Select the original received purchase and return one box. Enter a reason and post the return. Because this purchase is unpaid, the return reduces supplier debt instead of recording a cash refund. Supplier returns must use goods received on the original purchase.

  20. English transcript

    Open Bills and Payments and inspect the supplier bill. Record a ten dollar payment from the cash account, with a training reference, and confirm. The original purchase was forty dollars, the returned box reduced it by twenty, and this payment leaves ten dollars payable.

  21. English transcript

    Open Reports and Insights. Pharmacy Reports show expiry, stock availability and replenishment. Compare physical stock with released, unexpired stock. Then inspect Sales Reports and Supplier Report to review the transactions recorded in this walkthrough. Reports help reconcile daily work before closing.

  22. English transcript

    Inspect Chart of Accounts, Journal Entries and Trial Balance. Posted purchases, dispensing, returns and payments feed the existing accounting ledger. Trial Balance debits and credits should agree. Customer reports and receipt screens help review unpaid sales and collections. Use financial periods and tax settings according to your business setup.

  23. English transcript

    Open Administration. Staff Users and Roles and Permissions control access to modules and actions. The Audit Trail records activity. Pharmacy Settings contains the business identity, appearance and receipt configuration. Review these screens carefully and give each employee only the access their job requires.

  24. English transcript

    The daily sequence is catalog and units, supplier, purchase order, goods receiving, dispensing, payments, returns when needed, and reports. Check batch expiry and stock status, and use approved prescriptions where required. This walkthrough used fictional training records. You can replay any chapter from the accompanying chapter list.
